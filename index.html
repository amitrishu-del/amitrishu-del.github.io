01 / About

Where power electronics
meets plasma physics

I work on RF power electronics and the control of RF generators and plasma sources for semiconductor processing equipment. My focus is high-frequency power delivery, converter design, and the modelling and control problems that sit between power electronics and plasma physics.

Over the years I've designed RF power sources and generators, ultra-high-voltage high-frequency supplies, converters for energy storage and renewable integration, and high-voltage modulators for microwave transmitters. My path has run from India's DRDO, through a PhD and teaching at the National University of Singapore, to Advanced Energy and Lam Research in Singapore, California and now Bengaluru.

I like bridging deep theory with practical, simulation-driven engineering, and I'm always happy to connect with people working across RF, power electronics and plasma systems.
